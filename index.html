KNOW YOUR TEST

Practice with purpose.

CHSL LDC/JSA English typing: 35 WPM (about 10,500 KDPH), 10 minutes. Hindi standard: 30 WPM (about 9,000 KDPH). This version provides English passages.

SSC CHSL official notice ↗

A clear measure of progress

Correct KDPH = correctly aligned characters × 3,600 ÷ elapsed seconds. WPM = KDPH ÷ 300. Spaces and punctuation count.

Custom success requires the selected correct KDPH and at least 95% accuracy. Results are practice estimates, not an official SSC qualification decision. Exam evaluation rules and allowances depend on your current notice.

Keyboard drills cover printable keys. Shift is used for capitals and symbols; Enter, Tab and function keys are not passage characters.
